Privacy Policy — YT Analytics Reader

YT Analytics Reader is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool uses read-only Google OAuth scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read channel and video statistics, analytics reports and public comments.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show the owner their own metrics. It is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
